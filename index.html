Formal Phylogenetics

A catalogue of the methods, software and theorems of phylogenetics, arranged as the tree of the collection that formalizes them in Lean 4

The collection grows as a tree. At its root, lean4-phylogenetics holds the library that every formalization shares. Hubs of inference and of theory branch from the root, a sub-hub branches from a hub, and each hub sorts its catalog into families. Every tip below is an entry of a catalog. Point at a tip to name it, and at a hub to open its drawer.

FORMALIZED IN PROGRESS PARTLY COVERED TO DO Monospaced tags name a technique or a piece of software; tags in a serif name a result by its authors.

The page reads the registry of lean4-phylogenetics and the catalog of each hub, catalog/papers.toml, from GitHub each time it opens. Each formalized entry links to its repository, where the formalization and its audit are; the others link to the paper. More on the project: Phylogenetics in Lean 4.
